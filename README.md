# 📊 Vrinda Store Sales Analysis – 2022

## About the Project

This project is an analysis of **Vrinda Store's 2022 sales data using Microsoft Excel**.

The goal was to understand the store's sales performance, customer behavior, top-performing states and channels, and identify useful business insights.

I cleaned and organized the raw data, created Pivot Tables and charts, and built an interactive Excel dashboard using slicers.

## 🔍 What I Analyzed

Some of the main questions I wanted to answer were:

- Which months had the highest sales?
- Who buys more — men or women?
- Which age group contributes the most sales?
- Which states perform the best?
- Which sales channels generate the most sales?
- What is the order delivery, return, cancellation, and refund rate?
- Which product categories perform well?
- Who could be the ideal target customer?

## 💡 Key Insights

Here are some of the main findings from the analysis:

- **Women contributed around 64–65% of total sales.**
- **Maharashtra** was the top-performing state with approximately **₹2.99M** in sales.
- **Karnataka** and **Uttar Pradesh** were the next two top-performing states.
- Customers in the **30–49 age group** contributed around **50% of sales**.
- **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra** were the major sales channels, contributing around **80% of sales/orders**.
- Around **92% of orders were delivered**.

## 🎯 Target Customer

Based on the analysis, a potential target customer would be:

**Women aged 30–49, especially customers from Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.**

The main platforms to focus on would be **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra**.

Possible marketing strategies include:

- Targeted offers for women customers
- Personalized offers for the 30–49 age group
- Location-based promotions
- Seasonal discounts
- Channel-specific offers
- Retargeting existing customers
- Promoting high-performing products
